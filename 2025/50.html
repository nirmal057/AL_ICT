Zero trash!

This is a low cost way to compost kitchen waste:

Get three concrete well rings.

[Crop and Insert rings.jpg diagram from 4.PNG]

Put the daily collection of compostable waste from your kitchen in one ring. Cover the waste with a thin layer of soil. Repeat until the ring is full. When it is full, use the second ring the same way. Once the second ring is also full, use the third ring. But now you can take covering soil from the first ring as the content in it would have been turned to compost by now. When the third ring is full, the first one is likely to be empty so that you can repeat filling it with waste using soil for covering from the second ring.

Be happy that you are not burdening the town council anymore with kitchen waste and that you are making your own fertilizer!
